# CaseMate – Medical Record User Guide

## Purpose
CaseMate is designed to help patients and caregivers organize medical records in one place and make information easier to review.

## Medical Record Workflow
1. Open CaseMate and navigate to the medical record upload area.
2. Select a supported medical record file, such as an image or PDF, according to the options available in the application.
3. Review the information extracted or organized by the system.
4. Check important details against the original medical record and correct any inaccuracies when the application supports editing.
5. Keep the original record available for future reference.

## Privacy and Accuracy
- Avoid uploading medical records containing real patient information when testing the application.
- AI-generated or extracted information may contain errors and should be verified against the original document.
- CaseMate is intended to support record organization, not replace professional medical advice.

## Current Limitations
Some workflow steps may depend on the features available in the current Alpha version. This guide should be updated as functionality is tested and developed.
